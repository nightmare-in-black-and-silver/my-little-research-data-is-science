\subsection*{Dear Princess Celestia,}

An important part of understanding the magic of friendship is discovering where everypony comes from.

\textbf{Background:} Fimfiction.net is an online community centred on My Little Pony fanfiction, comprising users with diverse demographic characteristics, fandom histories, patterns of platform engagement, content preferences, and forms of social and creative participation. Despite the substantial activity associated with the community, a contemporary descriptive statistical profile of its users has not yet been established. This study will characterise the Fimfiction.net community through a cross-sectional survey of its users.

\textbf{Objective:} The primary objective is to describe the demographic characteristics, fandom histories, Fimfiction engagement, content preferences, community participation, and creative activities of survey respondents. Particular attention will be given to diversity and intersectionality, including the co-occurrence of disability, neurodivergence, LGBTQ+ identity, age, and other demographic characteristics alongside patterns of fandom and platform engagement.

\textbf{Methods:} The study will use a descriptive cross-sectional online survey administered to eligible members of the Fimfiction.net community during a defined survey period in 2026. The survey will collect information across demographic, fandom, platform-engagement, content-preference, social-participation, and creative-activity domains. Respondents will be permitted to select multiple applicable categories for characteristics where appropriate. The primary unit of analysis will be the individual survey respondent. Data will undergo predefined procedures for eligibility assessment, data cleaning, duplicate identification, response-quality assessment, coding, and handling of missing and non-substantive responses. Analyses will primarily consist of frequencies, percentages, distributions, subgroup and stratified descriptive analyses, cross-tabulations, and examination of relationships and co-occurrence between variables. Inferential statistical testing is not required for the primary descriptive analyses and, if undertaken, will be reported as supplementary or exploratory.

\textbf{Planned outputs:} Findings will be reported in an academic research paper accompanied by statistical tables, figures, and visualisations. Public-facing outputs will include accessible infographic and potentially interactive presentations of selected findings. Analytical code, survey materials, coding documentation, and other research materials will be retained and shared where ethically and practically possible.

\textbf{Preregistration status:} At the time of preparation of this analysis plan, the study has not yet been formally preregistered or registered with an external research registry.
