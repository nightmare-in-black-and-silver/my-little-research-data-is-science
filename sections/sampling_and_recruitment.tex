\section{Sampling and Recruitment}

\subsection{Sampling Strategy}

The study will use a \textbf{non-probability, voluntary response sampling strategy} to recruit participants from the Fimfiction.net community. Recruitment will be open to eligible members of the target population who encounter the survey invitation and choose to participate. The study will seek broad coverage across demographic characteristics, levels of Fimfiction engagement, fandom histories, and forms of community participation.

The limitations associated with voluntary response sampling, including potential self-selection and coverage bias, will be acknowledged when interpreting the findings.

\subsection{Recruitment Period and Method}

\sourceimage{1798204}{Watch as the GREAT and magical Trixie pulls a participant out of her hat!}

Participants will be recruited through online distribution of an invitation to participate in the survey. The primary intended recruitment setting is the Fimfiction.net community, with recruitment potentially extending to other online communities and platforms where members of the relevant population participate. Recruitment materials will describe the purpose of the study, eligibility requirements, and how prospective participants can access the survey.

Potential recruitment channels include Fimfiction.net itself and other MLP-related communities or platforms such as Discord, Reddit, Derpibooru, Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.

The survey will be administered during a defined recruitment period in 2026 and will remain available throughout the designated period, allowing respondents encountering recruitment material at different points to participate.

\subsection{Intended Sample and Population Coverage}

The study will seek broad coverage of the Fimfiction.net community rather than targeting a predetermined demographic composition. Recruitment will aim to obtain respondents with varied ages, countries of residence, fandom histories, generation preferences, levels of Fimfiction engagement, content preferences, and levels of community participation. Particular effort may be made to avoid recruiting exclusively from highly active writers or other narrow segments of the community.

The resulting dataset will be treated as a sample of survey respondents and not automatically as a statistically representative sample of all Fimfiction.net users.

\subsection{Stopping Rule}

Recruitment will end according to a prespecified stopping rule established before substantive analysis of the survey data. Decisions about extending or ending recruitment will not be based on whether the collected data produce particular descriptive or inferential findings.

\subsection{Quotas or Other Sampling Constraints, Where Applicable}

No demographic quotas or other fixed sampling constraints have currently been established. The intention is to permit eligible respondents to participate without requiring the final sample to reproduce a predetermined distribution of demographic or fandom characteristics.

\subsection{Potential Non-response Bias}

Potential non-response bias will be considered when interpreting the findings, particularly because voluntary online recruitment may result in systematic differences between people who choose to participate and members of the wider Fimfiction community who do not. The observed sample will therefore not be treated as automatically representative of all Fimfiction users.
