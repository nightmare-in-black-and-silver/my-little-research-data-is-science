\subsection{Brief Description of the Study}

This study will provide a survey of the contemporary Fimfiction.net user community, examining demographic and background characteristics, participation in the My Little Pony fandom, engagement with Fimfiction and other fandom platforms, and patterns of reading, writing, and community participation.

Findings will be presented in an academic paper and accompanying visual and interactive formats intended to make the results accessible while retaining methodological transparency.
