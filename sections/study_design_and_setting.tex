\section{Study Design and Setting}

\subsection{Study Design}

This study will use a \textbf{descriptive cross-sectional survey design} to characterise the contemporary Fimfiction.net community. Data will be collected from individual respondents through a structured online questionnaire administered during a defined survey period.

The study is primarily descriptive, with an exploratory component as described in Section~\ref{sec:objectives}. The primary unit of analysis will be the individual survey respondent.

\subsection{Study Setting}

The study will be conducted in the context of the online \textbf{Fimfiction.net} community, a web-based community centred on My Little Pony fanfiction. The primary study population will comprise individuals who use or participate in Fimfiction.net and who elect to participate in the survey. The survey will be administered online, allowing participation from respondents regardless of their geographical location, subject to the final eligibility and recruitment criteria.

Although Fimfiction.net is the primary setting of the study, the survey will examine respondents' engagement with the wider My Little Pony fandom across other online platforms and offline community settings. These may include Discord, Reddit, Derpibooru, Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, conventions, meetups, and other fandom spaces.

The study takes place within a fandom that spans multiple generations of the My Little Pony franchise and a range of online and offline communities. Respondents may therefore have substantially different fandom histories and patterns of engagement across generations and platforms.
