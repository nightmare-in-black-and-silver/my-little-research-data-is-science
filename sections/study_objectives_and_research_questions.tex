\section{Study Objectives and Research Questions}
\label{sec:objectives}

The primary objective of this study is to develop a contemporary descriptive profile of the Fimfiction.net community through a cross-sectional survey of its users. The study will examine who participates in the community, how respondents engage with the My Little Pony fandom and Fimfiction.net, what types of fiction and characters they prefer, and how they participate in the broader social and creative aspects of the fandom.

A particular objective is to describe the \textbf{diversity and intersectionality} of the community. Demographic characteristics such as disability, neurodivergence, LGBTQ+ identity, religion, age, country, education, and political orientation will be examined both individually and in combination where appropriate. The study will therefore describe patterns across multiple characteristics rather than attempting to construct a single homogeneous ``average'' Fimfiction user.

The primary descriptive research questions are:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{What are the demographic characteristics of respondents to the survey?}
\item \textbf{What are the patterns of fandom participation among respondents?}
\item \textbf{How do respondents engage with Fimfiction.net and the broader My Little Pony online community?}
\item \textbf{What are the fiction preferences of respondents?}
\item \textbf{What forms of social and community participation do respondents report?}
\item \textbf{What proportion of respondents participate in fanfiction writing, and what characteristics distinguish readers, writers, and respondents who engage in both activities?}
\end{enumerate}

The study will additionally examine the following secondary descriptive research questions:

\begin{itemize}
\item What proportion of respondents identify as a brony, pegasister, or other MLP fandom identity?
\item How strongly do respondents identify with the fandom?
\item What proportion of respondents read Everyone, Teen, and Mature-rated fiction?
\item What proportion of respondents report reading sexually explicit fiction (``clop''), and how frequently?
\item How likely are respondents to engage with individual Fimfiction tags?
\item What are respondents' preferences for male/female, female/female, male/male, non-binary, or other pairings?
\item What are respondents' preferences for cross-species and human/Equestrian pairings?
\item What are respondents' relative preferences for main cast, secondary characters, background characters, and original characters?
\item What other fandoms do respondents engage with?
\item How many fandoms do respondents participate in?
\item To what extent do respondents use generative AI in connection with fanfiction or other fandom activities?
\item What forms of AI assistance are used, including grammar correction, proofreading, brainstorming, story development, prose generation, artwork, and other purposes?
\end{itemize}

The study is primarily a \textbf{descriptive cross-sectional survey}. Its principal purpose is to describe the characteristics, behaviours, preferences, and experiences reported by respondents. The study is not designed around a predetermined causal hypothesis. It will also include an \textbf{exploratory component}, particularly in examining intersections between demographic characteristics and fandom behaviours. Exploratory findings will be used to identify patterns and potential directions for subsequent research rather than treated as causal or confirmatory conclusions.
