\subsection{Target Population}

The target population is \textbf{users of the Fimfiction.net community} who are eligible to participate in the survey. The study is intended to describe people who participate in the Fimfiction community rather than the broader My Little Pony fandom as a whole. The survey will seek to capture variation in demographic characteristics, fandom history, patterns of Fimfiction engagement, content preferences, social participation, and creative activity within this population.

\subsection{Inclusion Criteria}

Participants will be eligible for inclusion if they meet the final definition of the target Fimfiction.net population and voluntarily complete the study survey. Participation will be open to users with different levels and forms of engagement, including readers, writers, community participants, and individuals who primarily engage with Fimfiction through other aspects of the platform.

\subsection{Exclusion Criteria}

Responses will be excluded where they do not meet the predefined eligibility requirements, are determined to be duplicates, or are determined to be invalid or inappropriate according to the predetermined response-quality criteria. Exclusion will apply to the participant's complete response where the issue affects the validity of the submission as a whole.

Participants will not be excluded solely because they provide an unusual response, identify with an uncommon demographic category, select an unexpected combination of characteristics, or decline to answer a sensitive question.

\subsection{Unit of Analysis}

The primary unit of analysis will be the \textbf{individual survey respondent}. Analyses will use respondent-level data to describe the overall study population and relevant subgroups.
