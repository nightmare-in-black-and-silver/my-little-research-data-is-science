\section{Planned Outputs}

\subsection{Planned Research Publications and Reports}

The primary research output will be a formal academic paper reporting the findings of the descriptive cross-sectional survey. The paper will present the study methodology, survey instrument and measures, data-cleaning procedures, descriptive analyses, subgroup and intersectional findings, limitations, and interpretation of the results.

The study is intended to form part of a broader series of research into the Fimfiction.net community.

\subsection{Planned Public-facing and Interactive Outputs}

A public-facing version of the findings will be produced alongside the academic paper. This will take the form of a substantial blog post and associated infographic material designed to make the findings accessible to members of the Fimfiction and wider My Little Pony communities while retaining the underlying statistical results.

Where technically practical, the project will also include interactive visualisations allowing readers to explore different cross-sections of the surveyed population.

\subsection{Planned Presentation of Descriptive Findings}

\sourceimage{635262}{Buks!}

Findings will be presented through a combination of conventional statistical tables, charts, and infographic-style figures. The presentation will include an overall descriptive profile of the respondent population and more detailed examinations of demographic, fandom, platform, content, social, and creative characteristics. Intersectional findings will be presented where the available sample size permits meaningful description.

The visual presentation may combine statistical reporting with a strong My Little Pony visual theme. Illustrations and other visual assets used in the academic paper and public-facing outputs will be appropriately attributed, with their sources and usage information documented.

\subsection{Planned Data, Code, and Documentation Outputs}

The project will aim to produce a reproducible collection of research materials accompanying the final findings. Planned materials include the final survey instrument, analysis plan, variable and coding documentation, data-cleaning documentation, analysis scripts, derived-data procedures, statistical outputs, and documentation of deviations from the preregistered analysis plan.

The availability of respondent-level data will be subject to the privacy and disclosure considerations described in Section~\ref{sec:ethics}.
