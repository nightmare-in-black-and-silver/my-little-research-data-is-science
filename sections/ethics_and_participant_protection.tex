\section{Ethics and Participant Protection}
\label{sec:ethics}

\subsection{Ethical Oversight and Participant Protection}

This study is being conducted as an independent research project rather than under the auspices of a university or other institution with an established research ethics committee. No formal institutional ethics review has been obtained at the time of preparation of this analysis plan. The study will nevertheless apply safeguards appropriate to an anonymous online survey involving potentially sensitive demographic and fandom-related information.

Participation will be voluntary. Respondents will not be required to disclose identity-linked information, and sensitive questions may be skipped without penalty. The study will minimise collection of personal information and will report findings in aggregate to reduce risks of identification or community harm.

\subsection{Informed Consent Procedures}

Before beginning the survey, prospective participants will be provided with information about the purpose of the study, the nature of participation, the types of information requested, the voluntary nature of participation, the handling and publication of data, and the potential risks associated with sensitive questions. Consent will be obtained before substantive survey questions are presented.

Participants will be informed that they may discontinue participation before submission without penalty and may decline individual questions where the survey permits.

\subsection{Age Eligibility and Protection of Minors}

\sourceimage{2032129}{}

The study will not automatically exclude respondents under 18 years of age. Younger members of the Fimfiction.net community are relevant to the study's objective of describing the community as a whole and may therefore contribute to the general descriptive analysis subject to the final consent and eligibility procedures.

The survey will collect age information sufficient to distinguish relevant age groups while avoiding unnecessary precision where possible. The presence of respondents under 18 will not, by itself, result in collection of additional identifying or sensitive information.

The study will not investigate individual minors or attempt to identify respondents who may have accessed inappropriate material. Any age-related findings will be reported only in aggregate.

\subsection{Sensitive Content Handling}

The survey will include limited questions concerning Mature-rated and sexually explicit fiction on Fimfiction.net. These questions will be clearly identified as sensitive and will be optional.

The survey will ask whether respondents consume sexually explicit material on Fimfiction.net, but will not request descriptions of the material, specific sexual activities, individual works, or other unnecessary details. No question will request respondents to identify or disclose material that may be unlawful.

Specific categories of potentially illegal sexual material will not be included as survey response options or tag-preference items. The study will therefore measure broad participation in sexually explicit material on Fimfiction.net without soliciting potentially unlawful disclosures.

Any observed relationship between respondent age and reported consumption of sexually explicit material on Fimfiction.net will be treated as an aggregate community-level finding. Such findings may be discussed as potentially relevant to community safeguarding and content-access practices, without making a legal determination about the conduct of individual respondents or the platform.

\subsection{Data Privacy and Anonymity}

The survey will be designed to minimise collection of personally identifying information. Fimfiction.net usernames, email addresses, and other direct identifiers will not be required for participation, and the study will not seek to verify respondents' identities or link survey responses to Fimfiction.net accounts.

Sensitive demographic information, including disability, neurodivergence, LGBTQ+ identity, religion, and political orientation, will be collected only where relevant to the study objectives. Results involving small or unusual combinations of characteristics will be aggregated, combined, or suppressed where necessary to reduce re-identification risk.

Free-text responses will be treated as potentially identifying and will not be publicly released in identifiable form.

\subsection{Data Storage and Retention}

\sourceimage{1828956}{}

Raw survey data and analytical datasets will be stored securely with access restricted to the researcher and any subsequently authorised research contributors. Data will be retained only for as long as required to complete the analysis, reporting, and reasonable verification of the research findings.

Public release of respondent-level data will not occur where the combination of demographic, fandom, or free-text information creates an unacceptable risk of re-identification. Any dataset released publicly will be appropriately de-identified and assessed for disclosure risk before release.

\subsection{Recruitment Ethics}

Recruitment will be conducted through appropriate online community channels and will not involve deception, impersonation, harassment, or coercive contact. Recruitment materials will provide sufficient information for prospective participants to make an informed decision about participation.

Where distribution through Fimfiction.net or another community requires permission from site administrators or moderators, that permission will be obtained before recruitment through the relevant channel. Recruitment will not imply endorsement by Fimfiction.net, its administrators, or community moderators unless such endorsement has actually been provided.

\subsection{Risk Assessment}

The study is expected to involve no more than minimal risk, but some questions may cause discomfort because they concern identity, disability, neurodivergence, religion, political orientation, sexuality, Mature-rated fiction, or other sensitive aspects of fandom participation. Respondents may skip questions where permitted and may discontinue participation before submission.

No direct personal benefit is expected. The anticipated benefits are the production of a contemporary empirical description of the Fimfiction.net community and the generation of information that may be useful to community members, researchers, and site operators.

Information about appropriate support services may be provided where sensitive survey topics could reasonably cause distress.

\subsection{AI-related Questions}

Questions concerning generative artificial intelligence will address respondents' reported use of AI in connection with fanfiction or other creative activities. The study will not evaluate respondents' creative practices or require them to justify their use or non-use of AI.

Respondents will not be required to upload writing samples, submit private creative material, or provide copyrighted or proprietary content for analysis.

\subsection{Community Impact and Dissemination}

The study will seek to report findings accurately, respectfully, and without sensationalising characteristics or behaviours of the Fimfiction.net community. Particular care will be taken when presenting findings concerning small demographic groups, sensitive content, or characteristics that could contribute to stigma or harassment.

The final findings will be communicated through both the formal research report and public-facing visual and infographic materials. Where findings may have material implications for the Fimfiction.net community or its operation, the site owners will be provided with a copy of the substantive report before publication. Provision of the report will provide an opportunity for the site owners to review the findings and provide comment, but publication will not be contingent on their approval of the research findings.

Potential safeguarding implications identified by the data, including patterns concerning access to sexually explicit material among younger respondents, may be reported as community-level findings. The study will distinguish such empirical findings from any separate legal assessment.
