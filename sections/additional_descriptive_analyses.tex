\section{Additional Descriptive Analyses}

\subsection{Secondary and Supplementary Survey Domains}

Secondary and supplementary domains will provide additional descriptive context for the Fimfiction.net community. These include fandom identity, cross-platform engagement, participation in Fimfiction groups, convention and event participation, friendships and romantic relationships formed through the fandom, engagement in non-MLP activities with other fans, other fandom interests, writer-specific behaviours, story ratings, sexually explicit fiction, Fimfiction tags, character and pairing preferences, and use of generative artificial intelligence for creative or related activities.

\subsection{Distribution of Individual Survey Items}

The distribution of individual survey items will be described where useful for understanding the surveyed population. Categorical questions will generally be presented using frequencies and percentages for each response category, while numerical and ordinal questions will be summarised using appropriate measures of central tendency, dispersion, and distribution.

\subsection{Frequency, Intensity, Duration, and Contextual Dimensions}

Where the survey measures recurring or ongoing behaviours, descriptive summaries may distinguish between whether a respondent engages in an activity and the frequency, intensity, or duration of that engagement. Where appropriate, historical participation will be distinguished from current participation.

\subsection{Other Planned Descriptive Summaries}

Additional descriptive summaries may be used to characterise aspects of the community not covered by the primary demographic, fandom-engagement, content-preference, or subgroup analyses. These may include the number of platforms on which respondents engage with MLP content, the number of other fandoms reported, the number of Fimfiction groups joined, the number of conventions attended, and the number or types of creative activities undertaken.
